# Stepcast Privacy Policy

_Last updated: September 2026_

Stepcast does not collect personal data for itself. There are no Stepcast
accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no tracking of any kind, and the
developer runs no server that receives your data.

All data — your subscriptions, playback positions, bookmarks, downloads,
listening statistics and settings — stays on your device, with these
exceptions that only happen because you use them:

- **Backups.** Manual and automatic backups are written only to folders
  you choose. If Android's own backup (Google backup / device transfer) is
  on for your phone, Stepcast's settings and a copy of your library
  (subscriptions, listening progress, bookmarks — never audio) are
  included in that backup, stored by Google under your Google account like
  other apps' data. Sync credentials are never included.
- **Sync (optional, off by default).** If you set up sync, your
  subscriptions and listening progress are sent to the gpodder.net or
  Nextcloud server *you* enter, using the account *you* provide. Stepcast
  has no access to that server.
- **Chromecast (optional).** When you cast, the episode's audio address,
  title and artwork are sent to your Cast device, using Google's Cast SDK
  (part of Google Play services).

Network requests otherwise go to: the RSS feeds of podcasts you subscribe
to, the audio hosts those feeds point at, Apple's public podcast
search/charts API, and — when enabled in a build — the Podcast Index
search API (podcastindex.org) for discovery. Search terms are sent to
those directories to answer your search. These requests carry no
identifiers beyond what any HTTP request includes (plus a "Stepcast"
user-agent).

Crash reports are saved locally on your device and are only shared if you
explicitly use the "Share crash report" option.
